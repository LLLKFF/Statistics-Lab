# Global Macro Dashboard

## Purpose

The dashboard is the monitoring layer of Global Macro Lab.

It tracks key macroeconomic and financial variables to identify changes in economic regimes and market conditions.

---

## Dashboard Structure

### Growth

- GDP
- PMI
- Employment
- Consumption
- Investment

### Inflation

- CPI
- PCE
- Wage Growth
- Commodity Prices
- Inflation Expectations

### Monetary Policy

- Policy Rates
- Central Bank Communication
- Balance Sheets
- Market Pricing

### Interest Rates

- 2Y Treasury
- 10Y Treasury
- Yield Curve
- Real Yield
- Term Premium

### Liquidity

- Dollar Index
- Financial Conditions
- Credit Conditions
- Capital Flows

### Risk Assets

- Equity Index
- Volatility
- Credit Spreads
- Commodity Markets

---

## Research Usage

The dashboard does not provide trading signals.

Its purpose is to support macro regime identification and research decisions.
