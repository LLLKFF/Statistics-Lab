# Global Macro Data Dashboard v1.0

## Purpose

Global Macro Data Dashboard is the data layer of Global Macro Lab.

The objective is to provide a structured view of macro conditions and support research decisions.

## Core Dimensions

### Growth

- GDP Growth
- PMI / ISM
- Industrial Production
- Retail Sales
- Employment
- Corporate Earnings

### Inflation

- CPI
- Core CPI
- PCE
- Core PCE
- Wage Growth
- Inflation Expectations
- Oil and Commodity Prices

### Monetary Policy

- Fed Funds Rate
- FOMC Communication
- Dot Plot
- Balance Sheet
- QT/QE

### Interest Rates

- US 2Y Treasury
- US 10Y Treasury
- Yield Curve
- Real Yield
- Breakeven Inflation
- Term Premium

### Liquidity and Risk

- DXY
- Financial Conditions
- Credit Spreads
- Equity Risk Premium
- VIX

### Commodities

- Brent
- WTI
- Gold
- Copper

Research principle:

Data is used to explain macro mechanisms, not simply collect market prices.
