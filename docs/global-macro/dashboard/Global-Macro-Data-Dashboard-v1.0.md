# Global Macro Data Dashboard v1.0

## Purpose

Global Macro Data Dashboard is the data layer of Global Macro Lab.

The objective is not to collect market data mechanically, but to provide a structured view of macro conditions and support research decisions.

---

# 1. Growth Dashboard

## United States

Key indicators:

- GDP Growth
- PMI / ISM
- Industrial Production
- Retail Sales
- Consumer Confidence
- Nonfarm Payrolls
- Initial Jobless Claims
- Corporate Earnings

Research questions:

- Is growth accelerating or slowing?
- Is consumption sustainable?
- Are businesses expanding investment?
