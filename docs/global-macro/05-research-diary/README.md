# Global Macro Research Diary

## Purpose

Research Diary records the evolution of macroeconomic views over time.

It focuses on how information changes expectations, how markets reprice, and how research conclusions are updated.

---

## Writing Philosophy

A research diary is not a news summary.

It should answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- Through what mechanism does it affect markets?
- What assumptions support the current view?
- What evidence would change the conclusion?

---

## Structure

Each entry includes:

1. Market Observation
2. Macro Interpretation
3. Policy Analysis
4. Institutional Perspective
5. Asset Market Implications
6. Statistics Lab View
7. Follow-up Indicators
8. Post Review

---

## Research Principle

> Record the reasoning process, not only the final conclusion.

The value of macro research comes from continuous updating, validation, and correction.
