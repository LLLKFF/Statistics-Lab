# Macro Data Notebook

## Overview

Macro Data Notebook is the empirical research layer of Global Macro Lab.

The purpose is to transform macro narratives into measurable variables, reproducible analysis, and statistical research projects.

Core principle:

> Do not only observe macro events. Measure, test, and explain them with data.

---

## Research Workflow

Economic Question

↓

Data Collection

↓

Data Cleaning

↓

Exploratory Analysis

↓

Statistical Modeling

↓

Economic Interpretation

↓

Research Note

---

## Research Areas

### 1. US Inflation Analysis

Questions:

- Is inflation persistent or transitory?
- Which components drive inflation?
- How does inflation affect monetary policy?

Potential methods:

- Time series analysis
- Forecasting models
- Bayesian models

---

### 2. Federal Reserve Cycle Analysis

Questions:

- How does Fed policy affect markets?
- Are policy shocks identifiable?
- How does market pricing adjust?

Variables:

- Fed Funds Rate
- FOMC communication
- Treasury yields
- Dollar index

---

### 3. Yield Curve Research

Questions:

- Why do long-term yields move?
- Is the driver policy expectation or term premium?

Variables:

- 2Y Treasury
- 10Y Treasury
- Yield spread
- Real yield

---

### 4. Dollar Cycle Analysis

Questions:

- What drives dollar cycles?
- How does dollar liquidity affect global assets?

Variables:

- DXY
- Interest rate differential
- Global liquidity indicators

---

### 5. Gold Pricing Model

Questions:

- What determines gold prices?
- How do real rates and dollar cycles affect gold?

Variables:

- Gold price
- Real yield
- Dollar index
- Inflation expectation

---

### 6. Macro Regime Classification

Research objective:

Identify different macro environments and study asset performance under different regimes.

Potential methods:

- Hidden Markov Model
- State Space Model
- Clustering
- Machine Learning

---

## Output Standard

Each project should include:

- Research question
- Data source
- Methodology
- Statistical evidence
- Economic interpretation
- Limitations
- Reproducible code
