# 03 Global Macro Lab (New Core Module)

## Mission

Use statistical thinking and empirical analysis to understand global economic cycles, monetary policy, financial conditions, and asset prices.

## Research Philosophy

Global Macro Lab does not aim to predict short-term market movements without evidence.

The goal is to transform macro narratives into testable research questions.

Research chain:

Economic Condition

→ Policy Response

→ Financial Conditions

→ Capital Flow

→ Asset Prices

---
