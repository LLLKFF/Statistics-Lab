# Global Macro Research Framework v1.0

## Overview

Global Macro Lab is a new research module of Statistics Lab. It focuses on using statistical thinking and empirical methods to understand global economic cycles, monetary policy, financial conditions, and asset price dynamics.

Core philosophy:

> Macro research is not about predicting news. It is about understanding the transmission mechanism from economic conditions to policy responses, financial conditions, capital flows, and asset prices.

---

## 1. Research Framework

The core analytical chain:

Economic Condition

→ Policy Response

→ Financial Conditions

→ Capital Flow

→ Asset Prices

---

## 2. Macro Regime Analysis

The first question of macro research:

What economic environment are we currently in?

Key dimensions:

### Growth

- GDP
- PMI
- Employment
- Consumption
- Investment

### Inflation

- CPI
- PCE
- Wage growth
- Commodity prices
- Inflation expectations

### Policy

- Central bank stance
- Fiscal policy
- Regulatory environment

### Liquidity

- Money supply
- Central bank balance sheet
- Credit conditions

Typical regimes:

- Goldilocks
- Inflationary Growth
- Stagflation
- Deflationary Pressure

---

## 3. Monetary Policy Research

Core question:

How does central bank policy influence financial markets?

Focus areas:

- Federal Reserve
- ECB
- BOJ
- PBOC

Key concepts:

- Reaction Function
- Interest Rate Cycle
- QE/QT
- Forward Guidance
- Inflation Targeting

The market does not only trade economic data. It trades expectations of policy responses to economic data.

---

## 4. Interest Rate Research

Interest rates are the anchor of global asset pricing.

Key framework:

Long-term yield ≈ Expected Short-term Rates + Term Premium

Important indicators:

- 2Y Treasury Yield
- 10Y Treasury Yield
- Yield Curve
- Real Yield
- Breakeven Inflation
- Term Premium

Research questions:

- Why are yields rising?
- Is the driver monetary policy expectation or risk premium?
- How does the yield curve affect other assets?

## Methodological Principle

Statistics Lab does not aim to produce market predictions without evidence.

The objective is:

- Transform macro narratives into testable hypotheses.
- Use data to evaluate evidence.
- Document uncertainty and limitations.
- Connect economic mechanisms with empirical analysis.
