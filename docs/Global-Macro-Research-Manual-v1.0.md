# Global Macro Research Manual v1.0

## 1. Research Philosophy

Global Macro Lab aims to build a systematic framework for understanding the interaction between economic cycles, policy decisions, financial conditions, and asset prices.

The core principle:

> Do not predict headlines. Understand mechanisms.

Every macro question should be analyzed through:

Economic Conditions
→ Policy Response
→ Financial Conditions
→ Capital Flows
→ Asset Prices

---

## 2. Macro Research Architecture

### Layer 1: Economic Cycle

Questions:

- Is growth accelerating or slowing?
- Is inflation rising or falling?
- Is the economy above or below potential?

Main dimensions:

- Growth
- Inflation
- Employment
- Productivity

---

### Layer 2: Policy Environment

Focus:

- Central bank reaction function
- Fiscal policy
- Regulatory changes

Key questions:

- What problem is policymakers trying to solve?
- What constraints do policymakers face?
- How will policy expectations change?

---

### Layer 3: Financial Conditions

Monitor:

- Interest rates
- Yield curve
- Credit spreads
- Dollar index
- Liquidity conditions

Financial conditions are the bridge between macro economy and asset markets.

---

### Layer 4: Global Capital Flow

Key concepts:

- Dollar system
- Global financial cycle
- Cross-border capital movement
- Risk appetite

Important question:

Where is global liquidity flowing?

---

### Layer 5: Asset Pricing

Translate macro views into market implications:

- Equity
- Fixed income
- FX
- Commodity
- Gold
- Crypto

---

## 3. Macro Research Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the Event

What happened?

Examples:

- CPI surprise
- Fed decision
- Oil shock
- Fiscal announcement

### Step 2: Explain the Mechanism

Why did it happen?

Avoid simple correlation. Build transmission channels.

### Step 3: Assess Macro Impact

Evaluate:

- Growth
- Inflation
- Policy reaction

### Step 4: Evaluate Market Impact

Analyze:

- Rates
- Dollar
- Equity
- Commodity

### Step 5: Define Scenarios

Base case:

Expected path.

Risk scenario:

What could invalidate the view?

---

## 4. Research Standards

Every research note should include:

1. Clear hypothesis
2. Economic mechanism
3. Supporting evidence
4. Alternative explanations
5. Key indicators to monitor
6. Conditions that would change the conclusion

The goal is not prediction accuracy alone, but improving the quality of reasoning.
